Maya Assistant — Phone/Wevlo Build

This version uses Groovy Gradle files (build.gradle/settings.gradle) instead of Kotlin DSL (.kts).
This improves compatibility with phone/cloud builders that inject signingConfigs or other Gradle snippets.

Build task: assembleDebug
APK: app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk

If the builder asks for signing, choose its default/debug signing option.
